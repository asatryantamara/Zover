About

We control the build, start to finish.

Founded in 2021, Zover Construction has become one of Armenia's leading developers across residential and commercial projects. We oversee the whole process — from sourcing materials to handing over the finished building.

We produce our own construction materials, which keeps pricing competitive and quality in our hands. Combined with a skilled team and an extensive machinery fleet, that lets us deliver on time and on budget, whatever the complexity.

Our own production

Producing materials in-house means competitive prices and quality we verify ourselves — not whatever the market hands us.

Planning & engineering

Every square meter is planned for comfort and efficiency. Sound design up front is what makes the rest of the work pay off.
